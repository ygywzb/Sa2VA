\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented Sa2VA-Select, a compatible extension of Sa2VA that transfers learnable visual token selection to unified dense grounded image-video understanding.
By selecting tokens on the fused image/video+VP stream before the MLLM, Sa2VA-Select preserves Sa2VA's original segmentation-and-conversation interface while enabling token budget control.
Experiments show that the transferred selector remains effective in this unified setting, with the strongest trade-off consistently appearing around 30--40\% retention.
Qualitative and ablation results further indicate that the gains come from better suppression of distractors and from aligning soft training-time selection with hard inference-time pruning.
Overall, Sa2VA-Select provides an empirical step toward efficient unified dense grounding rather than a QA-only compression trick.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
We leave stronger backbones, finer budget sweeps, and more detailed token-level analysis for future work.
