\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented Sa2VA-Dev, which learns to retain visual features that best support \texttt{[SEG]} generation for dense grounded image-and-video understanding.
By training LIS/DiffTopK/CAS on Sa2VA's unified visual stream, we reduce the visual token budget while keeping the baseline task interface and improving the quality of segmentability cues used by SAM2.
Empirically, we observe a favorable accuracy--efficiency trade-off on Sa2VA-1B on dense grounded benchmarks and efficiency profiling, including improved overall multi-task performance at 30\% retention and reduced peak GPU memory.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.}
Our current study is limited to Sa2VA-1B and a small set of retention ratios.
Future work includes a controlled analysis of positional encoding choices under pruning (e.g., RoPE variants for spatiotemporal tokens), broader scaling to larger backbones, and a more comprehensive system-level profiling of latency under different decoding settings.
